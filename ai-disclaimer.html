AI Disclaimer

Important: AI-generated results can be incorrect. Always review the Sale Preview before confirming a transaction.

What AI does

AI Shop Manager may interpret natural-language or voice instructions and convert them into a proposed structured sale.

Possible errors

The AI may misunderstand product names, quantities, prices, customer names, payment amounts, or whether a payment is partial or complete.

Human confirmation

AI output is intended as an assistant, not an autonomous transaction authority. The user should verify the preview and correct it before selecting Confirm Sale. The application should update stock, payment and udhar records only after confirmation.

Professional advice

The service is not a substitute for accounting, tax, legal or financial advice. Important records should be independently verified where appropriate.
