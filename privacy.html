Privacy Policy

AI Shop Manager
Effective date: 11 September 2026
Last updated: 11 September 2026

This draft policy describes how AI Shop Manager may process information when users use the shop-management service. It should be reviewed and updated to match the final product, vendors and actual data practices before public launch.

1. Information we may process

  • User/shop account details such as name, email or phone number.
  • Customer details entered by the user, such as name and contact information.
  • Product, price, stock, sales, payment and credit/udhar records.
  • Voice input and/or text transcripts when voice features are used.
  • Technical and security information needed to operate the service.

2. Why we use information

We may use information to provide shop-management features, create AI-assisted sale previews, maintain stock and transaction records, provide support, improve reliability and security, and meet applicable legal obligations.

3. AI processing

Voice or text instructions may be sent to a configured AI service provider for interpretation. AI output can be wrong. The application is designed so that AI interpretation is a proposed sale and should require human review and confirmation before stock, payment or udhar records are changed.

4. Third-party providers

Depending on the final deployment, we may use cloud hosting, database, AI, authentication and security providers. The final published policy should identify material providers and explain relevant processing.

5. Voice data

If voice features are used, audio may be processed by the browser/device and/or a configured speech/AI provider. The final product must state whether recordings are stored, whether only transcripts are retained, and the applicable retention period.

6. Security

API credentials should be kept on backend infrastructure and must not be embedded in public JavaScript or an Android APK. Reasonable technical and organisational measures should be used to protect personal data.

7. Retention and deletion

Data should be retained only for the period needed for the stated purposes or as required/permitted by applicable law. Users may contact the service operator to request applicable data actions such as correction or deletion.
